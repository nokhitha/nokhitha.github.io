Delete your nokhitha account

nokhitha (نوخذة) · Kuwait's marine marketplace · abdulwahab@nokhithakw.com

Delete it yourself, in the app

This is immediate and permanent. You do not need to ask anyone.

  1. Open nokhitha and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is gone straight away.

If you no longer have the app

Email abdulwahab@nokhithakw.com from any address, with the phone number you used to sign in. No account or app needed. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Everything below is removed permanently and cannot be recovered:

  • Your account and the phone number it was created with
  • Your profile and display name
  • Every listing you posted, and all of its photos
  • Your conversations and all messages in them
  • Anyone you blocked, and any reports you filed
  • The registration that lets us send notifications to your phone

There is no undo. Deleting is instant and we keep no backup copy of your account to restore from. If you want your listings, save the photos first.

What is kept, and why

  • If you claimed the launch discount with your email address, that email sits on our mailing list separately from your account, so deleting the account does not remove it. Use the unsubscribe link on any email we send, or ask us and we will remove it.
  • Messages you sent to another person disappear from the app with your account. Nothing we can do reaches a screenshot somebody already took.
  • Anonymous crash reports may remain for up to 90 days. They record what broke, not who you are, and cannot be traced back to you once the account is gone.

حذف حسابك في نوخذة

من داخل التطبيق — فوري ونهائي:

  1. افتح التطبيق وتأكد من تسجيل دخولك.
  2. اضغط حسابي في الشريط السفلي.
  3. انزل إلى الأسفل واضغط حذف الحساب.
  4. أكّد الحذف. يُحذف حسابك في الحال.

إذا لم يعد التطبيق لديك: راسلنا على abdulwahab@nokhithakw.com مع رقم الهاتف الذي سجّلت به، وسنحذف الحساب خلال ٣٠ يوماً ونرد عليك بالتأكيد.

ما الذي يُحذف: حسابك ورقم هاتفك، ملفك الشخصي، جميع إعلاناتك وصورها، محادثاتك ورسائلها، قوائم الحظر والبلاغات، وتسجيل الإشعارات. الحذف نهائي ولا يمكن التراجع عنه.

ما الذي يبقى: إذا سجّلت بريدك الإلكتروني للحصول على خصم الإطلاق فإنه يبقى في قائمتنا البريدية بشكل منفصل — يمكنك إلغاء الاشتراك من أي رسالة أو مراسلتنا. تقارير الأعطال المجهولة قد تبقى حتى ٩٠ يوماً ولا يمكن ربطها بك.
